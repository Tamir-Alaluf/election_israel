# Product

## Register

product

## Users
General Israeli voters who want practical, trustworthy guidance before elections. They are often comparing options quickly across mobile and desktop, with limited time and varying familiarity with political terminology.

## Product Purpose
This product helps people make a more informed voting decision by presenting party and candidate information in a structured, understandable format. Success means users can compare key differences confidently, understand terms that affect their choice, and leave with a clearer personal decision.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, calm, and clear. The tone should respect civic responsibility, reduce noise, and help users focus on substance rather than emotional pressure.

## Anti-references
Avoid interfaces that feel like dense academic policy documents with heavy jargon and poor scanability. Also avoid sensational or manipulative framing that could undermine trust.

## Design Principles
1. Clarity over volume: surface the most decision-relevant information first.
2. Comparison is the core workflow: make differences between options obvious and fair.
3. Respect user autonomy: inform the decision, never push the decision.
4. Calm civic confidence: reduce anxiety through structure, plain language, and predictable interactions.
5. Progressive understanding: let users go from quick scan to deeper detail without friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline across core user journeys. Ensure readable Hebrew/RTL typography, keyboard navigation, clear focus states, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Use plain language where possible so content is accessible to users with different literacy and political familiarity levels.
